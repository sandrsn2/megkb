How the reports were sorted

  • Cause comes from the tenant, not from intake. intakeTag is a guess from the reporter's browser; the tenant's locale and booking form decide the cause (causes.ts).
  • The one-day reports are a separate bug. The desk notes mark five reports as one-day moves. CauseTable files them under their tenant's cause and only footnotes them, so its parser and hint rows count 379 and 33. Counted as a problem of their own, the numbers are 374, 33 and 5.
  • Subject lines hide the problem. Reports with the same subject have different causes, which is why merging by subject lumped them together.

Lessons

  • Merging by subject counts reports. It does not find causes. Read the tenant before you merge.
  • A footnote under a row is easy to miss. If a report is a different bug, give it its own row.
